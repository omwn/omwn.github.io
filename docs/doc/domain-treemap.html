WordNet Domain Treemap

Each rectangle represents an XWND domain, sized by the number of noun synsets assigned to it. Colour indicates the top-level branch of the domain hierarchy. Click any rectangle to zoom in; click the breadcrumb or the background to zoom back out. Also available as an icicle chart.

Data: 82,115 noun synsets from omw-en:1.4 mapped to ili_domains.tsv. Hierarchy from WordNet Domains 3.2 (Magnini & Cavaglià 2000) extended by XWND (González et al. 2012). JPC1 project.

Maintainer: Francis Bond
