WordNet Domain Icicle

Each column is one level of the XWND domain hierarchy, reading left to right from root to leaves. Height is proportional to the number of noun synsets assigned to that domain (including all descendants). Colour indicates the top-level branch. Click any cell to zoom in; click the root bar or breadcrumb to zoom back out. Also available as a treemap.

Data: 82,115 noun synsets from omw-en:1.4 mapped to ili_domains.tsv. Hierarchy from WordNet Domains 3.2 (Magnini & Cavaglià 2000) extended by XWND (González et al. 2012). JPC1 project.
